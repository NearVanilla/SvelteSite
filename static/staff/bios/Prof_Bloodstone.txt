Professor Bloodstone, the benefactor and current owner of the NearVanilla server, was once the go-to force behind the scenes—fixing issues, writing code, and developing plugins to enhance server life. These days, he’s taken on the role of a wise mentor to the Tech Team, guiding rather than grinding.

He’s so rarely seen in-game that it’s rumoured he hasn’t had a base since Season 2. If you do happen to spot him online, consider yourself lucky… or cursed, if you’re a sneaky Pete.
